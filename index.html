🌿 Phase guide
🩸 Menstrual (Day 1–5)
Your period. Rest, warmth, and gentle movement help. Iron-rich foods like spinach, lentils are great. Avoid caffeine if you feel crampy.
🌱 Follicular (Day 6–13)
Energy rises! Great time to start new projects, exercise more, socialize. Estrogen is building up — you'll feel lighter and more positive.
🌕 Ovulation (Day 14–16)
Peak energy, confidence & social drive. Fertile window is open. You may notice clear, stretchy discharge — that's completely normal!
🌙 Luteal (Day 17+)
PMS symptoms may appear: bloating, mood swings, cravings. Magnesium (dark chocolate, nuts), gentle yoga, and good sleep help a lot.
🍎 Nutrition by phase
During period
🥬 Spinach, 🫘 Lentils, 🍫 Dark chocolate, 🫚 Ginger tea, 🐟 Salmon — replenish iron and reduce inflammation.
Follicular & ovulation
🥦 Broccoli, 🥑 Avocado, 🫐 Berries, 🥚 Eggs, 🌰 Flaxseeds — support hormone production and energy.
Luteal (PMS relief)
🍌 Banana, 🥜 Almonds, 🍠 Sweet potato, 🫖 Chamomile tea — magnesium & B6 reduce bloating and mood swings.
💆 Self-care tips
When cramps hit
Warm water bottle on lower abdomen, gentle circular massage with coconut oil, ginger + honey tea. Avoid cold foods.
Sleep & mood
Progesterone in the luteal phase causes tiredness — 7–9 hours is key. Avoid screens 30 min before bed. Journaling helps mood regulation.
Exercise guide
Period: walking, stretching. Follicular: cardio, strength. Ovulation: HIIT, intense workouts. Luteal: yoga, pilates, gentle walks.
